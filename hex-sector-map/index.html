Lances

The number on each lance is its movement points. Pick an Action for each lance before moving; it sets how far the lance can go and whether it may end on an enemy lance's hex. Each hex entered costs 1 MP and a Rough hex 2 MP; entering a highway hex, or following a highway out of one, costs ½ MP. Impassible hexes can't be entered, and lances never move through an enemy hex.
